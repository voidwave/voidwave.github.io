Starwoven✦

Drag across matching stars to weave constellations before your moves run out. Short levels, one-thumb play, and a near miss on almost every loss. Try it on the phone below.

How it plays

  • Chain 3 or more matching stars. Diagonals count.
  • Close a loop back onto your own chain and every star of that colour clears.
  • Chain 6 or more and the last star becomes a Nova. Clear it to blast its 3×3.
  • Hit every goal before the move counter reaches zero.

The session shape

Each level lasts 60–120 seconds. Stars you earn fill a chest every 10★, so the reward always sits a level or two away and pulls the next level in. Novas and loops give surprise payouts, and big chains cascade, so a lucky clear feels earned.
